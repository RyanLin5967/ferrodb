=== WHAT WAS BUILT =====================================================================

`<db>.arena` is now `[image][tail record]*`. `alloc_arena` appends a 45-byte claim record and
fsyncs it; `free_arena` appends a 25-byte free record and fsyncs it. `replace_atomically` is
untouched and becomes the compaction path, firing when the tail passes `max(image/2, 4 KiB)`.

**The image format did NOT change.** Every `<db>.arena` on disk still opens, `key_order_in_image`
and `two_stores_in_the_same_state_checkpoint_byte_identical_images` are untouched, and no
`STATE_VERSION` bump was needed — a structural walker (`ArenaPageStore::image_len`) finds the
image/tail boundary instead. That was a deliberate trade: this file's own D85 note prices a version
bump at *"migrate every `<db>.arena` on disk"*, and the walker costs one allocation-free pass at
open time. It is pinned against `state_bytes` by a test whose fixture fills every variable-length
section, because a section left empty has its stride multiplied by zero and a wrong stride vanishes.

Two things were not obvious before writing it:

  * **The tail is an ORDERED log, so durable order must equal in-memory order.** `free_arena`
    returning range X and `alloc_arena` immediately re-claiming it, written down backwards, replays
    to X both on the free list *and* live in `extents` — two owners for one page range, and nothing
    about the file looks wrong. Full-image checkpoints could not have this bug, because each one
    publishes a coherent snapshot and the last writer wins. A new outermost persist mutex is held
    across the state mutation and the append on both paths.
  * **A full image made everything durable as a side effect, and a record does not.** `next_free`
    and the per-extent `recycled` list need no guard — `load_state` already clears `current` and
    D85 already marks every restored extent fill-unknown, *per extent* rather than per age of
    image, so an older image means more extents get that treatment, not weaker treatment. The
    **pending-free log** does need one, because nothing refuses to trust it and a lost entry is a
    page `drain_pending` never revisits. A version counter forces a rewrite when it has moved.

=== ⛔ DEAD OPTION 2 — THE CIRCULARITY CHECK THE DESIGN DEMANDED FIRST ==================

Recorded in full in `artie-research/SCALE-DESIGN.md` at commit 91c631c. In short, and read out of
the code at 52e1137 rather than recalled:

**The circularity does not bite, and the direction is the opposite of the guess.** `recover(&txn)`
is `cli/cli.rs:65`; `ArenaPageStore` is not constructed until `cli/cli.rs:100`. WAL replay reads
`{db}.wal` by LSN through `WalManager::read_record` — its own file, not a page — with no arena
floor registered and no arena store in existence. WAL replay does not need arena state to start.

**Option 2 is NO-GO anyway, on RETENTION.** `TxnManager::checkpoint` → `WalManager::truncate` is
`file.set_len(HEADER_SIZE)` plus a `base_lsn` advance (`wal/log.rs:759-762`): it physically
discards every record. The WAL keeps a record until the next checkpoint; an extent claim must stay
durable for the life of the extent. Putting the claim in the WAL erases it at the next checkpoint
while the extent is still live, and the next open re-issues the range — exactly the aliasing that
killed option A. Second, independent blocker: `base_page` would have nowhere to live, since
`reopen_from_checkpoint` deliberately takes it from the image and not from a caller.

**FSYNCS PER CLAIM: exactly 2.000 → 1.00, at every L.** A `replace_atomically` is four operations
and two fsyncs (write temporary, `sync_file`, rename, `sync_dir`); an append is two operations and
one, because it changes no directory entry and so has nothing for `sync_dir` to do.

**BYTES PER CLAIM: LINEAR IN L → FLAT.** The BEFORE column fits `24·L + 3000` bytes with **no
residual over four points** (15000 / 27000 / 51000 / 99000 predicted against 14992 / 26992 / 50993
/ 98993 measured). That is D79's 48 B-per-live-branch law arriving through a different counter —
24 rather than 48 because the image averages half its final size across a phase — so this run
**reproduces the prior finding rather than disagreeing with it**. The `map bytes` column agrees
too: 197,849 B at L=4000 against D79's 195,929, and the difference is exactly 1,920 = 40 × 48,
which is the 40 extra branches this run's 120 merges create over D79's 80.

The AFTER column is 122 / 161 / 142 / 167 B — a 1.37x spread across an 8x range in L, and
non-monotone, i.e. flat plus noise.

  total durable bytes over the four phases:  530 MB  →  1.19 MB    (446x)
  at L = 4000 alone:                      398,292 KB →   671 KB    (593x)

⚠ **Projection to 10^6 is ARITHMETIC, not data.** BEFORE: `sum(48i) = 24N²` = 24 TB, which is D79's
figure. AFTER: the compaction rule gives an amortised `45 + 48L/(24L/45) = 135` B per claim,
independent of L — corroborated to ~20% by the measured 167 B/claim at L=4000, which is an
independent counter and not a rearrangement of the same division — so ~135 MB, and ~10^6 fsyncs
where there were 2×10^6.

=== ⛔ THE EXIT CRITERION AS STATED COULD NOT BE EVALUATED ==============================

The criterion was: ON/OFF median-ms-per-cycle is *1.88 / 1.48 / 2.39 / 3.29 at L = 500 / 1000 /
2000 / 4000, RISING*, and D81 is done when it stops rising.

**It does not rise in the BEFORE arm on this box, so the criterion cannot discriminate.** Mean of
two rounds, `median ms/cycle`:

    L          500     1000     2000     4000
    BEFORE/OFF  83.7    224.0    175.9    166.6
    BEFORE/ON  181.4    167.0    176.5    101.7
    ON/OFF      2.17     0.75     1.00     0.61      ⛔ not rising — and not 1.88/1.48/2.39/3.29
    AFTER/OFF   96.4    185.1     99.0     75.0
    AFTER/ON   114.1    110.0    172.3    151.3
    ON/OFF      1.18     0.59     1.74     2.02      ⛔ noise, in the other direction

This is not a new surprise and it should not be presented as one. `bench/d81_bytes_vs_fsyncs.txt`
had already failed to reproduce D79's direction on the same code — *"the two runs disagree in
DIRECTION, which is the honest statement that the per-cycle median is not measuring the map at
these sizes at all"*. This run makes it unarguable: in the first four-arm pass
(`bench/d81_curve_raw.txt`) the two **OFF controls — the arm the change cannot affect — disagreed
by up to 2.4x**, which by this project's own rule voids the comparison outright.

**The instrument is the reason, and it is diagnosable rather than mysterious.** A measured cycle is
`BEGIN AGENT SESSION` + 4 UPDATEs + `MERGE` and claims exactly ONE extent, so the free-space map is
a small share of what the median times. The instrument was therefore extended — identically on both
sides, from one commit cherry-picked onto both branches — to time the **park** phase, which is L
branches, one claim each, and nothing else.

=== METHOD, AND WHAT MAKES THE TWO HALVES COMPARABLE ====================================

* **One `measure-lock.sh` hold for all eight arms**, so no cell is separated from another by a
  window in which the fleet did something different. A sidecar touched the lock's owner file while
  — and only while — the run's pid was alive, because the lock reclaims an owner older than 45
  minutes on the assumption it is dead, and eight arms take longer than that.
* **Two binaries, one instrument.** `git diff a7b8c50:examples/d75_live_branch_scaling.rs
  0f596da:examples/d75_live_branch_scaling.rs` is **empty**; the only difference between them is
  `src/branch/arena.rs`. Both were rebuilt from clean committed trees, so each table above is
  headed by the binary's own `build_provenance()` naming its commit — the first pair were built
  from dirty trees and were rebuilt for exactly this reason.
* **Round 2 runs the four arms in reverse**, so every cell sits at a different point in the window.
  ⚠ It does NOT reverse the L axis *inside* an arm — that is the harness's own loop — so L and
  position-within-arm remain confounded. Named, not solved.

=== UNIT-LEVEL EVIDENCE, AND THE MUTATIONS THAT PROVE IT IS EVIDENCE ====================

14 new tests in `src/branch/arena.rs`, 3 in `src/storage/atomic_file.rs`. Each of the arena ones
asserts a non-empty tail or `appends > 0` *before* it asserts anything else, so a suite that never
entered the append path fails instead of passing quietly. Two carry explicit controls: the
corruption test checks that the same damage in the LAST record still opens (or the refusal is
blanket, not a rule), and the pending guard is made to fire and then shown not to fire.

**Five mutations applied to 53a6b66, each reverted after measuring:**

  mutation                                             caught by
  pending-log guard → `false`                          parking_a_page_forces_the_next_claim… (+1)
  `image_bytes == 0` guard → `false`                   10 tests, incl. a_reopened_store_rewrites…
  mid-tail corruption refusal → unreachable            a_bad_record_with_records_behind_it…
  `image_len` extent stride 28 → 24                    6 tests, incl. the walker pin
  claim replay's free-list removal deleted             a_free_and_an_immediate_reclaim_of_one_range…

A sixth was found by the suite rather than planted: leaving `live_pages` out of the claim record
made a restart report 0 live pages after 7 allocations, which is why the record carries it as an
absolute while `reserved_pages` is carried as a delta.

=== WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY =============================================================

⛔ It does not say the persistence penalty is gone at 10^6. Everything above L=4000 is arithmetic.
⛔ It does not say the cycle-median curve improved. On this box that number is noise in both arms.
⛔ It does not close the reap-heavy case by measurement. `free_arena` now appends too, so the shape
   is right, but the d75 workload builds its runtime without a reaper, so no `free_arena` fired in
   any arm above. The free record is covered by unit tests only.
⛔ It says nothing about a cluster. Every arm is single-node; an authority change deliberately
   forces a full rewrite and no measurement here exercises that path.
