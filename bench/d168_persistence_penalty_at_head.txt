=== THE DISCRIMINATING-POWER CHECK — why "flat" is not just noise ===================

The OFF arm is noisy: within-arm spread between blocks reaches **1.45x** (ON reaches 1.23x), which
is the same order as the effect. A flat curve read off a noisy instrument can be a non-result, so
the question is not "is the noise smaller than the effect" but **"would the alternative hypothesis
have produced a visibly different number?"**

  If the pre-D81 growth were still present, ON at N=4000 would read 156.4 / 3.29 = **47.5** f/s.
  Measured ON at N=4000: **115.1** (blocks 127.1 and 103.1).

47.5 sits far outside both blocks. **The instrument can discriminate, and it excludes the pre-D81
shape.** The LEVEL (~1.4x) is soft and is quoted as approximate; the SHAPE is the robust claim, and
the SHAPE is what was pre-registered.

=== CONTROL — held =================================================================

`len B/branch` is the load-independent column and is **identical across all four arms**:
4104 / 4100 / 4098 / 4097 at N = 500 / 1000 / 2000 / 4000, OFF and ON alike. The two arms ran the
same workload; the timing difference is the map's durability and nothing else. (D80's own control
was the same column at 4104 both ways.)
