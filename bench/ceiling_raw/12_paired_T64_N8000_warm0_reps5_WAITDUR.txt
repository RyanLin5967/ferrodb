⭐ WAIT and DURABLE ARE THE DIRECT ANSWER TO 'WHAT IS THE REMAINING TIME', AND
   THEY BEAT THE CENSUS. Every thread's cycle is wait -> hold -> durable, so
   WAIT + HOLD_TOT + DURABLE = T * S_eff (D123 §2b's closure identity, checked in
   the `close` column). WAIT is time spent BLOCKED ACQUIRING `logical` — measured
   in nanoseconds by the phase clock, not inferred from a collision count, and
   immune to the barging blind spot that makes contended/op under-report.
   ⇒ at L3, WAIT >> DURABLE means the gap IS threads blocked on the lock.
   ⇒ at L3, DURABLE >> WAIT means the gap is the disk, and outcome 1 is dead for
     a reason no collision count could have shown.
   D123 §2b measured this at L0 ONLY (T=64: WAIT 6.84 ms > DURABLE 5.35 ms). The
   L1-L3 rungs have never been decomposed, which is the hole this fills.
