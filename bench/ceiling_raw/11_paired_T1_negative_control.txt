⭐ WAIT and DURABLE ARE THE DIRECT ANSWER TO 'WHAT IS THE REMAINING TIME', AND
   THEY BEAT THE CENSUS. Every thread's cycle is wait -> hold -> durable, so
   WAIT + HOLD_TOT + DURABLE = T * S_eff (D123 §2b's closure identity, checked in

   immune to the barging blind spot that makes contended/op under-report.
