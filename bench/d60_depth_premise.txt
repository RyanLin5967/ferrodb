=== READING ===
Premises 1 and 2 of SCALE-DESIGN D60, three runs, depth 1 -> 250 (31x past the cap of 8):

  read of a ROOT row at the chain's leaf:   9.4 -> 9.2 us, 7.5 -> 7.2 us, 9.8 -> 9.4 us   FLAT
  fork (median of every fork since the last checkpoint): ~2-3.5 us from depth 8 to 250    FLAT
      (depths 1-4 are warm-up: the first forks pay allocator and cache cold-start)

So the design entry's hypothesis holds on the two paths that would make a depth limit legitimate:
a read is one descent of the leaf's OWN root (CoW), and a fork copies no ancestry. This is ZFS,
not qcow2 -- a qcow2 backing chain costs a probe per level on every read, which is exactly the
case where a depth limit (and `qemu-img commit`) is the right answer. Here it is not.

Premise 3 (the one depth-dependent recursion, has_live_children over a chain of reaped interior
nodes) is not measured by this probe: its recursion depth is the length of a reaped chain, which
the u8 depth field and the cap bound today. It is answered by the fix itself -- the recursion is
made iterative, and a test drives a reaped chain far past 255 that must neither overflow nor
answer wrongly.
