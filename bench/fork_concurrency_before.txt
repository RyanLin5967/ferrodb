INTERPRETATION (written after the numbers, not before):
  x1.08 at 8 threads and x0.92 at 64 is FLAT. Adding 64 concurrent forkers produced no more forks
  per second than one. That is the D6 premise check confirmed as a measurement rather than as a
  reading of the source: `TableBranchCatalog::fork` holds `self.logical` across `commit()`, and
  `commit()` fsyncs, so the forks serialize on the mutex and each pays a private disk round-trip.

  THIS IS WHY THE PLANNED MEASUREMENT WOULD HAVE LIED. `SCALE-NEXT` originally said "measure group
  commit". Group commit batches CONCURRENT commits. Against this code it would have returned x1.00
  and the conclusion on the page would have been "batching does not help an agent workload" -- a
  false statement about batching, caused by a mutex. The split has to come first.

  The 64-thread arm being slightly SLOWER (x0.92) is the mutex convoy, and it is the expected sign.
