================================================================================================
6. INSTRUMENT, LOAD, AND WHAT IS NOT TRUSTWORTHY HERE
================================================================================================

* Probe perturbation MEASURED, not assumed: -1.20% (warm=20k) and -1.83% (warm=0), 5 reps each,
  ON/OFF order alternated. Inside the pre-registered +/-2%. The first battery's single-shot
  +87.95% does not reproduce; that cell was n=1 and its OFF value (2796.6) was the outlier, not
  its ON value — the reading had inverted which cell was anomalous.
* The probe adds ~13 clock reads inside the section. That is common-mode between T=1 and T=64, so
  it DILUTES the 64/1 ratios toward 1.0. The inflation figures are therefore understated, not
  manufactured.
* `Instant::elapsed` quantises to 41.67 ns here. Every figure is a sum over >=8,000 samples
  (>=4,500 ticks for a 0.19 ms section). No single-operation duration is reported anywhere.
* LOAD. Lock held for every timed run. `load_at_acquire=4` for the re-run; no CAVEAT line printed
  (threshold is 8). But load RAMPED to 11.05 during 10_f1_warm20k and that arm is the one that came
  out noise-dominated. The measure lock excludes other MEASURERS, not builds or suites, so "lock
  held" is not "box quiet" and is not claimed to be. EVERY duration here is an UPPER BOUND.
* ⭐ WHAT IS NOT TRUSTWORTHY: any absolute millisecond figure, and the warm=20k F1 arm. What IS
  trustworthy: the counters (f/sync, syncs x f/sync = N), the COUNT of paired F1 draws above
  1.10x (17 of 18),
  and ratios of two durations taken by ONE instrument inside ONE process (U(hold), the 64/1
  column, per-upsert cost at fixed k) — which is where every conclusion above rests.
* A tree-size confound exists in F1 and is bounded rather than removed: stubbed levels insert fewer
  keys, so their trees grow more slowly inside the timed window. The depth-sensitive phases
  (core + envelope + free_scan) total ~0.022 ms of a ~0.19 ms section, so even a 20% swing in them
  moves F1 by ~2%, against an effect of 105%.
