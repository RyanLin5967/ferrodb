⇒ **F1 DOES NOT FIRE, IN BOTH REGIMES.** The falsifier required L3/L0 within +/-10% of 1.00x.
**17 of 18 paired draws are above 1.10x** and the combined median is 2.00x. No estimator, no
averaging across arms — the count of individual draws carries it.

⇒ AND THE CONTAMINATION BIASES THE RATIO THE SAFE WAY. warm=0's reps 5-8 ran while the box
degraded (L0 collapsing to 2412 / 1043 / 303 / 1220 forks/sec) and their ratios are HIGHER
(2.90-6.58x), because interference hurts L0 more than L3 - L0 spends 21x longer inside the section
where the interference lands. So the clean reps' **1.90x is a floor, not a midpoint**, and the
honest headline number is the warm=20k median of **1.86x** on an uncontaminated run.

  THE VOID GUARD, BY CONSERVATION. `syncs` falls 465 -> 250 as the section shortens. That is group
  commit batching more forks behind a shorter section, not lost durability, and the two are told
  apart by syncs x f/sync = 8000 = N, which holds EXACTLY at every level in every run.

1c. ⛔ I REPORTED THIS ARM AS "F1 UNDECIDED" AND THAT WAS THE WRONG INSTRUMENT, NOT A WRONG ARM.
Earlier I ran warm=20k UNPAIRED and reported "F1 undecided at this N", because within-level spreads
of 51-139% swamped level differences of 14-54%. That was the correct call for the summary I was
looking at. But the same regime, measured PAIRED, decides cleanly at 1.86x. The identical 9 reps

Spreads of 95-110%, non-monotone in L2 - and yet every paired draw from those very numbers is
>= 1.66x. **The ratio-of-medians and the median-of-ratios are not the same quantity when the box
drifts, and only the second answers F1.** Logging the reversal here, where the original call was
made: the data was always sufficient; my summary statistic was throwing the answer away.

That is the same defect as section 0's reciprocal and section 2e's 1.42x, and all three are one
rule: **a ratio is evidence only when both sides come from the same instrument and the same
moment.** Reciprocals share both and prove nothing; cross-regime ratios share neither; a
ratio-of-medians across a drifting box shares the instrument but not the moment.
