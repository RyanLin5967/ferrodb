⇒ **F1 DOES NOT FIRE.** The falsifier required L3/L0 within +/-10% of 1.00x. **0 of 9 reps are at or
below 1.10x, and the smallest ratio observed is 1.66x.** No estimator, no averaging — every single
draw refutes it.

⇒ AND THE CONTAMINATION BIASES THE RATIO THE SAFE WAY. Reps 5-8 ran while the box degraded (L0
collapsing to 2412 / 1043 / 303 / 1220 forks/sec) and their ratios are HIGHER (2.90-6.58x), because
interference hurts L0 more than L3 — L0 spends 21x longer inside the section where the interference
lands. So the clean reps' **1.90x is a floor, not a midpoint.**

  THE VOID GUARD, BY CONSERVATION. `syncs` falls 465 -> 250 as the section shortens. That is group
  commit batching more forks behind a shorter section, not lost durability, and the two are told
  apart by syncs x f/sync = 8000 = N, which holds EXACTLY at every level in every run.

Spreads of 95-110% against level differences of 54-87%, non-monotone in L2. On that reading the
arm is "F1 undecided" — which is what I correctly reported for the earlier warm=20k arm, and it was
the right call on the wrong instrument. **The ratio-of-medians and the median-of-ratios are not the
same quantity when the box drifts, and only the second one answers F1.** That is the same class of
error as the reciprocal in section 0: a quantity that looks like a comparison but shares a term
with the thing it is compared against.
