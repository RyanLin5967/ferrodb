⛔⛔ D101 CORRECTION — 2026-09-21. THE NUMBERS BELOW TIMED AN IN-MEMORY STUB.
`examples/d55_agent_read_scaling.rs` built a `ServerContext` over an `AgentRuntime::with_catalog`
on a real `TableBranchCatalog` sidecar, and then ran every statement on `Session::new()` — which
constructs its OWN `AgentRuntime::new()`: an in-memory branch catalog, `storage: None`, a private
effect log and private branch state. So the SHARED arm was NOT shared: each of the N threads had
its own private runtime, and the `ONE ServerContext` label describes a context none of the timed
statements consulted.
⚠ THIS IS LOAD-BEARING FOR THIS FILE SPECIFICALLY. The whole point of the shared arm is
contention on one `AgentRuntime`'s `Mutex<State>`; with a private runtime per thread there is no
such contention, so the scaling shown here is closer to the PRIVATE control than to the shared
arm it is labelled as. The x1.44-at-1-thread reading, and the honest note that the pushdown
bought far less than expected, are unaffected — they are single-threaded.

The numbers are NOT deleted and they are not noise: they are a real measurement of a real code
path. They are simply not a measurement of the path this file's headline names.
Guard that now refuses this configuration: `src/agent_sql/designated.rs` (commit ca61e38).
Harnesses corrected: commit fc443c4.  Re-run on the corrected harnesses: `bench/d101_rerun.txt`.

THE HONEST READING
------------------
Commit b53e80e's subject says "O(table) becomes O(log N) per read". On 5000 rows that should be
an order of magnitude at one thread. It is x1.44. So EITHER the pushdown did not happen (the
planner did not turn `id = k` into an index probe on this table -- the correct-but-slow mutant the
commutation test explicitly cannot see) OR it happened and the base scan was never the dominant
cost: 2216 stmt/s is ~451 us per statement, and something else is spending most of that.

Under the project's bar this is MICRO until proven otherwise, and the commit subject over-claims.
That is corrected in the ledger and design record in the same pass, not left standing. The
pushdown is still correct (tests/d55_pushdown_commutes.rs, mutant killed) and is kept; what is
withdrawn is the complexity-class claim, pending a profile.
