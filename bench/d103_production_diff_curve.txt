Subject: AgentRuntime::page_changeset_with_cost, the call DIFF <branch> reaches.
Question: does the production path cost O(delta · log_m N) now, or O(N)?
delta is held at exactly 4 changed rows; N is the only axis.

Instruments:
  visited   DiffCost::visited — nodes the synchronised descent DECODED, which on
            that path is also every node it read. THE CLAIM.
  skipped   subtree pairs found equal by page identity and abandoned unread.
  walked    TreeDiff::pages_walked — walk_pages(base)+walk_pages(head), what the
            path this replaced must enumerate before it can prune. THE CONTROL.
  examined  TreeDiff::pages_examined — the old counter. It reports the DECODE
            half only, which is why an O(N) operation read as cheap.

Both paths were asserted to report the same 4 changes at every N; a row that did
not would have aborted the run rather than been banked.

      N   nodes  depth | visited  skipped  changes |     walked   examined
                       |                           |  (control)   (the lie)
-------  ------  ----- | -------  -------  ------- |  ---------   ---------
   1000      67      2 |      10       62        4 |        134          10
   4000     278      3 |      12       70        4 |        556          12
  16000    1151      3 |      12       82        4 |       2302          12
  64000    4607      3 |      12      130        4 |       9214          12
 256000   18234      4 |      14      141        4 |      36468          14

N grew 256x (1000 -> 256000).
visited grew 1.4x (10 -> 14), tracking DEPTH 2 -> 4.
control  grew 272x (134 -> 36468), tracking N.

The old counter, for contrast: examined 10 -> 14, which is flat and true and says
nothing at all about what that path cost.

PRE-REGISTERED OUTCOME 1: the production path is O(delta · log N).
